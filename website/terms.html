The service

Saiph Cloud runs the connection between your approved Saiph devices, so your Android phone and Windows or Linux computers work together without you running a server. The Cloud page describes the current features and what the service can and cannot see. A Saiph account and an approved device are needed; paying does not approve devices or unlock content.

Calling the service a beta describes what you are buying; it does not reduce your statutory rights, including withdrawal within 14 days and remedies if the service does not work as described.

Free trial

When a free trial is offered, a new Saiph account can start one free trial of Saiph Cloud from its account page. It lasts for the number of days shown there (normally 14), needs no card and costs nothing. The trial is the full beta service on the same terms as a subscription, apart from price.

One free trial per person: a trial is tied to your Saiph account and to your verified email address, and is not available again with another account or an alias of the same address, or to someone who has already subscribed. We may limit trials to a number of places and stop offering new ones at any time; a trial you have already started always runs to its end.

When the trial ends, Cloud access pauses until you subscribe. Nothing is charged automatically and nothing is deleted: your account, approved devices and encrypted data are kept, and your devices reconnect once you subscribe. If you subscribe during the trial, your free days are kept and the first payment is taken when the trial ends.

Price and payment

A subscription costs €9.99 a month or €89.99 a year. That is the total: we are not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added, and there are no setup or cancellation fees. You subscribe on your account page and pay through Stripe, which handles your card details; we never see them. Checkout asks you to accept these terms and makes clear you are agreeing to pay. Stripe emails a receipt, and your account page shows your plan and renewal date.

Renewal and price changes

Your subscription starts straight away (if you subscribe during a free trial, the first payment is taken when the trial ends) and renews automatically at the end of each month or year, charging the same payment method, until you cancel. If we change the price, we will email you at least 30 days before it applies to your next renewal, and you can cancel before then without charge.

If a renewal payment fails, Cloud access continues for 3 days while you update your payment method with Manage billing; after that it pauses until payment succeeds. If a payment is disputed with your bank, Cloud access is paused while the dispute is open and comes back if it is resolved in our favour.

Cancelling

Use Manage billing on your account page, or email us, to stop future renewals at any time. Access continues to the end of the period you have paid for, and nothing more is charged. Ordinary cancellation does not refund the current period, except as set out below. Signing out, uninstalling an app or removing a device does not cancel a subscription.

You can also email us. The refunds page has the details and a model withdrawal form.
