Refunds and cancellation.

How to stop renewals, withdraw within 14 days, and what happens if something goes wrong. Last updated 15 October 2026.

Stop future renewals

Use Manage billing on your account page, or email support@usesaiph.com. Renewal stops at the end of your current paid period, you keep access until then, and there is no cancellation fee. Stripe confirms the cancellation and the date access ends. This does not refund the current period; for that, see withdrawal below. Signing out, removing a payment method or uninstalling an app does not cancel a subscription.

Withdraw within 14 days for a full refund

You can withdraw from your subscription within 14 days of subscribing, without giving a reason. If you subscribed during a free trial, the 14 days start on the day of your first payment, when the trial ends. A free trial itself costs nothing and needs no cancelling: when it ends, Cloud pauses until you subscribe. We refund everything you paid for it, even if you have already used the service, with no deduction and no fee.

The quickest way is Withdraw from contract here on your account page (account.dev.usesaiph.com/account/withdraw). Sign in, check the refund shown and press Confirm withdrawal. Your subscription and Cloud access end straight away, the full amount goes back to your original payment method, and the page records the date and time your withdrawal was received. Stripe also emails a refund receipt. Banks usually show the refund within 5 to 10 business days.

You can also withdraw by email or post using the details on the contact page. To meet the deadline, send your statement before the 14 days end. We refund within 14 days of receiving it, using your original payment method.

Service problems and other refunds

Withdrawal is separate from your rights if the service is faulty, missing or not as described. Those rights continue after the 14 days and may include having it put right, a price reduction, or ending the contract with a refund. Email us with what went wrong. If we stop providing Cloud without a breach on your part, we refund the unused part of your prepaid period.

Outside these cases, ordinary cancellation does not refund time already paid for, but we look at every request fairly. See the Cloud terms and the CCPC's consumer guidance.
