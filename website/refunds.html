Refunds and cancellation.

Future Hosted renewals, consumer withdrawal and service-problem refunds.

Draft · 11 October 2026. No live Hosted subscriptions are offered. These proposed rules require approval, working trader contact details and verified cancellation/refund procedures before checkout opens.

Stop future renewal

The proposed ordinary cancellation stops renewal at the end of your current paid period, with access through that period and no cancellation fee. Use the verified billing portal or published trader contact. Keep the cancellation confirmation and effective date. Cancellation of future renewal alone does not request immediate withdrawal or a refund. Account deletion, sign-out, removing a payment method or uninstalling an app does not substitute for cancellation.

Withdraw from an online service contract

EU consumers generally have 14 days from agreement of an online service contract to withdraw without a reason. To meet the deadline, send an unambiguous withdrawal statement before the period ends; the model below is optional. The proposed Saiph policy refunds the full initial payment for withdrawal within that period, even if you requested the service to start immediately. No early-start usage deduction or withdrawal waiver is proposed. Any longer statutory withdrawal period remains available.

Refunds owed following withdrawal will be initiated without undue delay and no later than 14 days after the trader receives the withdrawal notice, using the original payment method unless you expressly agree otherwise, with no refund fee. Your bank may take additional time to display the credit. Withdrawn service access ends as part of closing the contract, and future renewal must be stopped.

The quickest way is Withdraw from contract here on your account page (https://account.dev.usesaiph.com/account/withdraw). Sign in, check the refund shown and press Confirm withdrawal: the subscription and Hosted access end straight away, the full amount is refunded to your original payment method, and the page records the date and time received. Stripe also emails a refund receipt. This informational page does not submit a withdrawal request itself.

Service problems and other refunds

Withdrawal is separate from remedies for a faulty, missing or misdescribed service. Mandatory consumer rights continue after the cooling-off period; depending on the issue they may include a remedy, price reduction or termination and refund. Report the issue and order reference through the verified contact channel. The proposed policy refunds the unused prepaid period if the trader ends the service without your breach.

Outside withdrawal, a statutory remedy or an expressly approved refund, ordinary end-of-period cancellation does not automatically refund elapsed service. No blanket “all payments non-refundable” rule applies. See the Hosted terms. The final checkout and confirmation must display consistent policies.
