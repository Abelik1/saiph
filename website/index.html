Requirements & sources Checked 16 October 2026
  1. Apple Continuity connects compatible Apple devices. Phone notifications use iPhone Mirroring, which Apple currently lists as unavailable in the EU. See Apple feature requirements and Continuity and regional availability.
  2. Phone Link uses Windows; its Android and iPhone features differ. Cross-device clipboard works on selected Android models. File sharing requires Windows 11 and Android 10 or later; calling uses Bluetooth. See Phone Link requirements, clipboard and file sharing and calls.

Saiph Cloud includes notification viewing/dismissal, text and image clipboard, a recent-photo gallery after phone opt-in, sending photos/files, one-to-one SMS replies, new texts, and call controls. Turn on Texts and calls on the phone and grant its Android permissions. Notification replies, MMS pictures and group replies are not yet supported on Cloud; they work with a self-hosted Hub. Call audio stays on the phone in both modes. These are beta features, with full physical-device acceptance still pending. See Cloud features and the self-hosting guide.
