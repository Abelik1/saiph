Why Saiph

The seamless setup shouldn't need one brand.

“A friend told me the Apple ecosystem can't be beaten, so I built one that works on everything.”

— Alexander, creator of Saiph

Copy on your phone, paste on your laptop. See your phone's notifications on the screen you're at. Send a photo across without a cable. Apple made this feel normal, but only if every device comes from Apple. Saiph brings the same everyday flow to Windows, Linux and Android, so your next phone or computer can be whatever suits you.
