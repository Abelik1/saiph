# Community conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Explain disagreements with evidence, welcome people
with different experience, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, stalking and publication of
another person's private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project-managed spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict access
or ban participants according to severity and repetition. Maintainers are subject
to the same rules and should recuse themselves when directly involved.

Report conduct problems using GitHub's **Report content** control and, for threats
or harassment, GitHub's [abuse reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not publish private evidence in a public issue. Maintainers should handle
reports discreetly and share personal details only when needed to address harm.
