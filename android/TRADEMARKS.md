# Names and branding

Saiph and the Saiph logo identify the official project and its releases. No
registered-trademark status is asserted here. The source-code licences do not
grant permission to present a modified distribution or independent hosted service
as the official Saiph product.

You may describe compatibility, credit the project, and accurately identify
unmodified official releases. Modified distributions and independent services
must use distinct product names and branding and clearly describe their changes
and lack of affiliation. Preserve source attribution and licence notices.

This naming policy does not restrict the freedoms granted by the code licences.
Upstream names and marks belong to their respective owners.
