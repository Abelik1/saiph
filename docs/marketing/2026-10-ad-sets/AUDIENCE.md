# Who to target: audience brief for the first Saiph Cloud ads

Research note, 28 October 2026. Figures are cited with their source and date;
recommendations are marked as such.

## The buyer, in one line

Owns an **Android phone** and sits at a **Windows (or Linux) computer** most days,
is **25–44**, and can justify €9.99/month for removing daily friction.

Desktop (September 2026): Europe is **77.3% Windows** and **4.6% Linux**.
Germany is 76.6% Windows and **6.5% Linux**, the strongest Linux market checked.
Worldwide (June 2026) Linux was about 4.4%.

**What it means.** An ad shown to everyone in Ireland or the UK reaches iPhone
owners about half the time. Meta and TikTok both let you restrict delivery to
**Android devices only**. Use that from day one: it stops paying for impressions
Saiph can't serve, and national Android share then mostly sets audience size and cost.

## 2. Age: your 20–40 guess is close, but shift it older

- **Young adults lean iPhone.** MetaFacts finds iPhone use is highest among
  18–24-year-olds in every country it surveyed. That includes Germany, where
  Android dominates overall.
- **US teens are about 87% iPhone** (Piper Sandler, fall 2025). Young American
  audiences are a weak fit.
- **Android's share rises with age.** Older US data (Mercator) puts 18–34 at 58%
  iPhone, but 35–54 at 56% Android. Japan in 2026 shows the same pattern: young
  adults prefer iPhone, while from the 40s up an Android model leads.
- **Instagram's biggest age group is 25–34** (33.3%), then 18–24 (29.7%)
  (Statista, July 2025, via SocialPilot).
- **€9.99/month needs disposable income.** That favours people who are working,
  not most 18–22-year-olds.

**Recommendation:** core **25–44**. Run 18–24 only as a separate cell in
Android-heavy markets (Netherlands, Germany), where young Android users still
exist in volume. Test 45–54 later. That group is Android-leaning and pays, but
it's lighter on Reels.

## 3. Who inside that group (from the product, not yet from data)

| Slice | Signals you can target or speak to | Best set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office and hybrid workers | Job interests, productivity apps, Windows | A (workday) |
| Students and recent graduates | Study, university interests; 18–24 cell only | B (Ctrl+V) |
| PC builders, gamers, Linux users | PC hardware, Linux, open-source interests | C ("Enough.") or a Linux cut |
| Phone-Link-style users | Interests in Samsung Galaxy, Windows | A or B |

Interest targeting is weak on Meta these days. Broad targeting with Android
devices only, ages 25–44, usually works better, and the creative does the
selecting. Sets A, B and C are built to attract different slices on their own.

## 4. What ads cost, roughly

No reliable 2026 Europe-specific Reels benchmark was found. Planning ranges:

- Instagram Feed median about **$5 CPM** worldwide, with Reels and Stories
  slightly cheaper (Emplifi Q2 2026, via AdsUploader). The worldwide median
  includes cheap markets.
- US-targeted Instagram campaigns run about **$15 CPM** (Hootsuite citing Bïrch).
- Expect Western Europe somewhere between, and confirm in Ads Manager's
  estimate for each country before setting budgets.

**Illustrative budget maths** (assumptions, not benchmarks): at €8 CPM, €300
per set buys about 37,000 impressions. That's enough to compare hooks (3-second
views) and click-through. It's not enough to compare trial starts. At 1%
click-through and 3% of visitors starting a trial, it would produce about 11
trials per set, too few to call a winner. So judge round one on attention and
clicks, and judge on trials only once a set has earned more budget.

## 5. Recommended first test

| Setting | Choice |
| --- | --- |
| Markets | Ireland, United Kingdom, Netherlands (English creative) |
| Devices | Android only |
| Ages | 25–44 |
| Placements | Instagram and Facebook Reels and Feed |
| Structure | One ad set per creative set (A, B, C), equal budget, same landing page and offer |
| Read | 3-second view rate and outbound CTR at ≥1,000 impressions per ad; trial starts once a set scales |
| Next | German-language versions for Germany if a set wins, plus a Linux cut for the Linux slice |

**MISSING:** actual budget and loss limit, real CPMs for these countries from
Ads Manager, and any customer data (who signed up in the beta, their ages and
setups). The beta sign-ups, if their country and device are known, are the best
check on this brief.
