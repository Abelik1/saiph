# Positioning and promotion copy

Promotion drafts for owner review, 14 October 2026. Landing-page copy updated
16 October 2026. Nothing here is posted or scheduled.

## The message

Saiph aims to give people the seamless phone-and-computer experience of the
Apple ecosystem (clipboard, notifications and files moving between devices,
later texts and calls) on the devices they already mix: Windows, Linux and
Android. You should not need every device from one company to get it.

Origin line, owner-approved wording only:

> A friend told me the Apple ecosystem can't be beaten, so I built one that works on everything.

Do not invent further quotes or attribute other words to the owner or the friend.

## Rules for any copy

- Claim only what exists. Today: clipboard, notifications and photos/files
  between an Android phone and Windows or Linux computers, on a self-hosted Hub
  or the invite-only Hosted beta (text clipboard there). Saiph is in development;
  there is no public release or download yet.
- Texts, calls and webcam on Hosted are in source only, without physical
  acceptance or a release. Say "coming" or "on the way", never "now".
  Self-hosted texts and calls may be mentioned only as the landing page does.
- Don't say open source as if the code were public: the repository is private
  until publication. "Open-source licensed (AGPL/GPL), source publication planned"
  is accurate.
- Apple, iPhone, Mac and Continuity are Apple's trademarks. Use them only for a
  factual comparison ("like Apple's Continuity"), never in a product name,
  hashtag-as-brand, logo or imagery suggesting affiliation or endorsement. On
  pages and long posts add the note the landing page uses: "Apple and Continuity
  are trademarks of Apple Inc. Saiph is an independent project, not affiliated
  with or endorsed by Apple." Other names and marks belong to their respective
  owners; see [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md).
- Link to `https://usesaiph.com`. Don't link the private repository, beta
  invites or account site in public posts.
- Check [release status](RELEASE_STATUS.md) and
  [website launch gates](../website/LAUNCH.md) again before posting; update the
  copy if what's shipped has changed.

## Landing page

- Hero: **Your devices. Connected.**
- Lede: Clipboard, notifications and files across Android, Windows and Linux.
- Keep the interactive device graphic, followed directly by the download buttons.
  Firebase is the primary Android install option; the standalone APK is secondary.
- Omit the origin quote, Why Saiph, feature-card grid and separate security pitch.
  Keep pricing compact and link to the Hosted and self-hosting detail pages.
- Below downloads and pricing, compare current Apple Continuity, Microsoft Phone
  Link and Saiph capabilities in one table. Highlight Windows/Linux support and
  free self-hosting, retain competitors' strengths, and qualify Hosted versus
  self-hosted features. Link dated primary sources; exclude unreleased additions.
